## Delivery discipline

Use a short isolated branch, atomic conventional commits, exact-head review,
the complete applicable checks, protected delivery, and verification from the
resulting default branch. Preserve independent review findings and failed
controls. Do not put unrelated changes in a mechanism commit.

Each capability is introduced as one reviewable sequence: model/ID/scenario and
a red test; its complete definition; positive and negative verification;
representative planted defect; regenerated conformance. The model is the sole
authority. Tests may contain independent literal expectations; they must not
be a second implementation copied into the runtime.

## Conventional commit boundaries

`feat(geometry)`, `feat(route)`, `feat(flock)`, `feat(relation)`,
`feat(admission)`, `feat(learning)`, `feat(tokenizer)`, `feat(artifact)`,
`feat(serving)`, `feat(memory)`, `feat(conversation)`, `feat(model)`,
`ci(pages)`, and `docs(evidence)`. A commit records an independently reviewable
change; the prefix itself establishes no acceptance.
